# Publisher facts still needed

Do not infer these answers from a GitHub organization, profile name or domain.
This worksheet is preparation, not an attestation.

- Verified personal or business publisher name and responsible contact.
- Grok form: first name, last name, work email, organization name, lowercase handle.
- ChatGPT country targeting: selected countries or explicit all-available choice.
- Commerce: whether the service is free or paid; what can be purchased and where.
  Confirmed capability boundary: plugin tools do not place orders or process payments.
- A private working support channel for recovery, privacy requests and abuse reports.
- Actual model/email/hosting processors and relevant processing locations.
- Retention periods and deletion handling for accounts, intentions, contacts, audit
  records and backups; do not promise immediate or complete erasure without support.
- Service eligibility, age limits, operator terms and effective dates.
- Dedicated reviewer login/access and the desired demo recording host.

Draft support/privacy/terms pages are in policy-drafts/. Their publication and
legal commitments require the operator's decisions. No draft URL is placed in
plugin metadata. Public source Issues are available for non-sensitive package bugs;
they do not establish a private account-support or deletion process.
